case study

Two weeks to ten minutes.

Juno's insurance intake, rebuilt as a guided quote journey.

ClientJuno
EngagementEmbedded, 12+ months
Team2 designers
SectorMed spa insurance

the error state

Juno had 20 years of reputation and a sales process that leaked money. Every lead took 1 to 2 weeks to close, and every document change bought another billed envelope from a third party tool. The brief: quote in minutes, coverage in days, stop paying twice for the same form.

constraints

  • 01Same information, new journey. Every field underwriting required had to stay
  • 02One quarter to design and build the quote journey
  • 03Built in Bubble, so every pattern had to be buildable in a no code tool

what we found

The judgement calls, mapped

We mapped how Juno's team decided which plan suited a customer, then turned each of those judgement calls into a step in the journey. Flow, wireframe, shipped.

01User flow

Every judgement call the sales team made, mapped as a step, with the error and recovery paths beside it. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, or open the board in Figma.

02Wireframe

Four stages, one question at a time, with the assistant and the tip slot placed before any styling.

03Final design

the tradeoff

Owning the signing platform ended a $100k a year bill, but Juno now maintains it, compliance included, work a third party used to carry. The payoff: it bends to Juno's process instead of Juno bending to it.

what the team owns now

Juno runs its own signing platform, a Bubble build whose copy their team updates in house, and a style guide covering colour, type and core elements.
