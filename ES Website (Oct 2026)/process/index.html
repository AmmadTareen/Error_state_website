The whole engagement, published. You will know what the first two calls look like before you book either one.

Step 1 30 minutes before any contract

Intro call

We cover what is broken, what it is costing you, and whether a design system or an embedded engagement is the right shape. If it is not a fit, we say so on the call rather than sending a proposal you do not need.

  • 01An opinion on the problem, not a sales deck
  • 02A straight answer on whether we are the right team
  • 03A scope and a price range if we are
