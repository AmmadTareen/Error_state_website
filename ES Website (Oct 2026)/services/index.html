scope

What we do not do

Saying it here saves us both a call.

Not a dev shop

  • We hand off to your engineers
  • Specs, states and a walkthrough session
  • If you need the build, we point you at people who do it well

Not a brand studio

  • Product interface and systems
  • Not logos, not campaigns
  • Not brand strategy

Not hourly staffing

  • We work to outcomes on a cadence
  • Capped hours, not timesheets
  • A body in a seat by the hour is the wrong shape
