Privacy

This site collects no personal data directly. There is no contact form, no email capture and no newsletter.

Analytics

We measure page views and clicks on the booking link in aggregate, without cookies that identify you. No analytics provider is connected until we say so here.

Booking

The booking page embeds a Google Calendar appointment schedule. When you book, the details you enter go to Google and to us, under Google's own privacy terms. We use them to hold the call and to follow up about it, nothing else.

Fonts

Typefaces load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.
